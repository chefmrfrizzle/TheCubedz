# Code of Conduct

We want rigorous disagreement without personal hostility.

Contributors should:
- critique claims, methods, assumptions, and evidence rather than people;
- distinguish speculation from demonstrated results;
- welcome corrections and negative results;
- avoid credential-based dismissal while still respecting domain expertise;
- disclose conflicts of interest that materially affect scientific claims;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, or targeted abuse.

Maintainers may remove content or participation that undermines safe, evidence-driven collaboration.
