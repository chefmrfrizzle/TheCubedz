# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want rigorous disagreement without personal hostility.

Contributors should:

- critique claims, methods, assumptions, code, and evidence rather than people;
- distinguish speculation, computation, reproduction, and experiment;
- welcome corrections, retractions, and negative results;
- respect domain expertise without using credentials as a substitute for evidence;
- communicate uncertainty honestly;
- disclose material conflicts of interest;
- help beginners ask questions without lowering scientific standards;
- protect private vulnerability and incident information.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, doxxing, stalking, or targeted abuse;
- personal attacks disguised as scientific criticism;
- deliberate fabrication of evidence, citations, identities, or reproductions;
- coordinated manipulation of votes, stars, or discussion to imply scientific validity;
- publishing private security or personal information without permission;
- persistent disruption after a moderator asks for a change in behavior.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the repository's available private contact path. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove contributors when necessary to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should distinguish good-faith error or disagreement from deception, harassment, or repeated unsafe behavior. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
